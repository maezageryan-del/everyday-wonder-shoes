## The entry

Walking home is the most ordinary trip there is — and the one your feet
already know by heart. The line gives the knowing to the shoes instead of
the walker, then flips who is leading whom in the last four words. No
lift, no kettle, no shadow, no "as if": the surprise is the phrasing
itself, and every word is doing a job.

> My shoes remember the way home better than I do, and some evenings I only follow them.

86 characters, one sentence, one read.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to `entry.txt` in the output directory
(`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`), with no trailing newline, and
prints it exactly as written.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
